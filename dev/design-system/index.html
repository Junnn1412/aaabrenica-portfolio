How to review this page

  • Check the page at 320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440, and 1920px.
  • Tab through interactive specimens — links, buttons, form controls — confirming visible focus and that .btn never moves when it receives focus (the hover lift only ever applies on genuine pointer hover).
  • Toggle your OS's reduced-motion setting and confirm every transition/transform stops.
  • Toggle forced-colors/high-contrast mode and confirm every control, especially native checkboxes and radios, stays usable.
  • Zoom to 200% and confirm no horizontal scrolling appears.

Capability cards (PF-032)

.capability-card is CSS-only — there is no JS render function or content-data module yet. solutions.js/home.js are still placeholders, so there is no real production template to call a renderer from; the same precedent as .btn/.tag/ .media-frame. The six cards below are literal, hand-authored markup using the real compiled component classes — not a live render call — and only exist on this dev-only page.

The six descriptions are representative/provisional component copy, not final production marketing content — final category copy is decided when a real page composes these cards (PF-041/050+). All six link to /solutions/ (the one existing, approved route) purely to demonstrate the stretched-link pattern navigating somewhere real; it is not a hint at final information architecture, and no route content changes as part of PF-032.

The six approved capabilities

  • Custom Business Systems

    Purpose-built internal tools designed around how your team actually works.

  • Workflow & Process Solutions

    Turning manual, error-prone approval and reporting processes into reliable, trackable systems.

  • Corporate Websites

    Professional, fast, and easy-to-maintain websites that represent your business well.

  • WordPress Development

    Custom WordPress builds and improvements for teams that need a familiar, editable platform.

  • Existing-System Improvements

    Modernizing or extending systems that no longer match how the business runs today.

  • Support & Maintenance

    Ongoing support so systems stay reliable long after launch.

Additional specimens

Two demo-only cards, not counted among the six approved capabilities above — they exist purely to prove two markup paths the real content doesn't happen to exercise.

  • Demo: long title and description, no icon

    Demo: a deliberately long description to prove the card grows to fit its content instead of truncating it, and that a card with no icon renders correctly with the icon slot simply absent rather than leaving a blank space where one would otherwise sit — this sentence keeps going for a while specifically to stress-test the wrapping behavior at every one of the six required review widths, from 320px up to 1920px.

  • Demo: non-interactive (no destination)

    Demo: no .capability-card__link element is present in this specimen's markup at all, so — driven purely by that absence, not a modifier class — it renders with no stretched link, no arrow, no hover lift, no active feedback, and no focus ring. A static card must never look clickable.
